Product Category Performance

Identifying margin variations across core product categories

Category Sales ($) Profit ($) Margin
Office Supplies $551,369 $89,525 16.2%
Technology $712,265 $75,303 10.6%
Furniture $660,704 $59,249 9.0%

Category Drivers:

  • Office Supplies: Highest profit contributor ($89.5k) despite lowest sales volume, benefiting from solid base margins and manageable shipping expenses.
  • Technology: Dominates total sales ($712k), but overall margins are squeezed by high unit costs and freight fees.
  • Furniture: Yields the lowest margin (9.0%) primarily impacted by heavy weight shipping and container options.

Strategic Action Items

Data-backed recommendations to improve margins and streamline profitability

  • Restructure Southern Territory Discounting:
    Audit promotional discounts and carrier pricing in the South region to eliminate unrecoverable losses.
  • Optimize Furniture Shipping Container Mix:
    Review freight arrangements for bulky items (Chairs, Tables) to lower the shipping cost ratio.
  • Scale Office Supplies Cross-Selling:
    Bundle lower-margin technology sales with high-margin office supplies to increase ticket profitability.
